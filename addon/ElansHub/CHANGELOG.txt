1.0.0 First version: shares your character, class, level and zone with the Lodge

1.1.0 Adds the /rl shortcut for /reload (moved here from Elan's Hunter Helper)

1.2.0 Also shares race and gender (for avatars) and whether you are still logged in, so the hub stops showing the wrong character after you log out or switch

1.3.0 Adds a tiny status strip in the top-left screen corner (32 coloured squares, 4 pixels each) so the Hub sees your character instantly, without /reload. Turn it off with /ehub pixel off.


1.4.0 Adds a minimap button, a settings window (/ehub) and a quick emote wheel: hover or click the small icon for 8 emotes (Thank, Cheer, Wave, Hello, Bow, Laugh, Applaud, Dance by default, configurable). /ehub diag records emote API facts.

1.5.0 Richer presence for the Hub: the status strip gets a second row with your zone, XP %, rested state, group size, instance and a few flags (in combat, dead, AFK, resting). It never reads health, power, buffs or cooldowns. Older Hubs ignore the extra row. Updates on zone, combat, group and XP events, throttled to twice a second.

1.6.0 Optional "Emotes" chat tab so /e emotes and text emotes (/dance, /thank, the emote wheel) no longer fill General. On by default, per character, out of combat only. Switch in /ehub settings or with /ehub emotes on|off; turning it off restores General exactly.

1.7.0 New settings window with a tab list on the left (General, Emote wheel, About) like Elan's Hunter Helper; /ehub wheel opens the Emote wheel page and the window remembers the last page. Removed the Emotes chat tab - emotes are back in General (a one-time cleanup puts them back where 1.6.0 took them from, and closes the tab if the Hub made it).

1.8.0 Addon health for Elan's Outpost: errors of this addon are kept in the saved variables (ElansHubDB.bugs), and on the first login after an update one quiet /ehub diag run is stored (about 10 s after login, out of combat) with a one-time chat hint to /reload so the Outpost can check it.
